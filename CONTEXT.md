# 2048 Policy Learning

This project studies whether an open-weight model can learn a strong 2048 policy from a TD-learning expert and reliably reach the 2048 tile through environment play.

## Language

**Teacher Policy**:
The trained TD-learning policy that supplies expert action judgments for 2048 board states.
_Avoid_: Teacher model, TD model

**Teacher Policy Corpus**:
A fixed collection of Teacher Policy trajectory states and action judgments used to seed training and calibrate rewards without crossing trajectory or symmetry boundaries between splits.
_Avoid_: Static dataset, Teacher dataset

**Teacher Core**:
The immutable, train-only subset of the Teacher Policy Corpus used as a stable reference across experiments.
_Avoid_: Validation set, replay buffer

**Corpus Stratum**:
An exclusive Teacher Policy Corpus category: Late State takes precedence over Hard State, and all remaining states are Natural States.
_Avoid_: Class, label

**Late State**:
A Corpus Stratum state whose largest tile is at least 512.
_Avoid_: End game, terminal state

**Hard State**:
A non-Late Corpus Stratum state with at most four empty cells, at most two legal actions, or an ambiguous Teacher Policy action margin.
_Avoid_: Losing state, difficult sample

**Natural State**:
A Corpus Stratum state that is neither a Late State nor a Hard State.
_Avoid_: Easy state, random state

**Teacher Margin Scale**:
The median strictly positive difference between the top two Teacher Policy action scores in the training split, used to scale Action Quality Reward and serialized as `tau`.
_Avoid_: Temperature

**Symmetry Orbit**:
The set of board states equivalent under the eight rotations and reflections of the square; a single canonical identity keeps equivalent states within one corpus split.
_Avoid_: Augmented sample, trajectory

**Trajectory Lineage**:
The identity that associates a corpus state with its complete source trajectory so all states from that trajectory remain in one split.
_Avoid_: Chat history, sample index

**Student Policy**:
An open-weight model trained to choose a move from a 2048 board state by learning from the Teacher Policy.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, game model

**Policy Distillation**:
Training the Student Policy to reproduce the action judgments of the Teacher Policy.
_Avoid_: TD training, pretraining

**Policy Reasoning Trace**:
Reasoning generated by the Student Policy before it selects an action. It is part of the Student Policy's own GRPO rollout, not an explanation supplied by the Teacher Policy.
_Avoid_: Teacher reasoning, teacher rationale

**Policy Response**:
A Student Policy completion containing a Policy Reasoning Trace followed by exactly one proposed 2048 action.
_Avoid_: Teacher trace, action label

**Teacher-guided GRPO**:
The first training stage, where groups of Student Policy responses are rewarded according to the Teacher Policy's judgment of their proposed actions. The Teacher Policy does not supply or evaluate the Policy Reasoning Trace.
_Avoid_: CoT distillation, supervised fine-tuning

**Environment GRPO**:
The second training stage, where the Student Policy is rewarded from its actual 2048 environment outcomes rather than Teacher Policy agreement.
_Avoid_: Teacher-guided GRPO, imitation learning

**Action Quality Reward**:
The per-board Teacher-guided GRPO reward derived from the Student Policy action's regret relative to the best Teacher Policy action. Regret is scaled by a margin statistic calibrated from the Dynamic Board Pool, with a best-action bonus and penalties for Policy Failure; it does not reward the content or length of the Policy Reasoning Trace directly.
_Avoid_: Action accuracy, CoT reward

**Environment Reward**:
The Environment GRPO reward dominated by 2048 Success, supplemented by tile and game-score progress relative to the rollout's start state, and reduced by terminal penalties. Teacher Policy scores do not evaluate actions in this stage.
_Avoid_: Teacher score, evaluation metric

**Training Episode**:
An environment interaction sequence that ends at game over or when the Student Policy first produces a 2048 tile. Early termination at 2048 bounds training cost without changing the success objective.
_Avoid_: Evaluation game, prompt

**Student-visited State**:
A board state encountered while the current Student Policy interacts with the environment, including states created by its own mistakes.
_Avoid_: Teacher state, synthetic board

**Dynamic Board Pool**:
The evolving Teacher-guided GRPO board collection that begins with stratified Teacher Policy trajectories and periodically incorporates teacher-scored Student-visited States.
_Avoid_: Static dataset, replay buffer

**Policy Failure**:
A rollout or evaluation game terminated because the Student Policy produced an invalid response format or proposed an action that does not change the current board. The environment does not mask, replace, or retry the action.
_Avoid_: Game over, low-quality action

**Direct-action Policy**:
The control Student Policy that emits only an action and does not generate a Policy Reasoning Trace.
_Avoid_: Base model, non-thinking result

**Reasoning Policy**:
The experimental Student Policy that emits a Policy Reasoning Trace before its action.
_Avoid_: Teacher reasoning, CoT dataset

**Markov Policy**:
A Student Policy that receives only the current board state for each decision. The trainer may retain the full trajectory for learning, but prior boards, actions, and reasoning traces are not part of the policy observation.
_Avoid_: Chat history, trajectory prompt

**Start-state Curriculum**:
The Environment GRPO schedule that begins short rollouts from reachable late-game and hard states, then increases both rollout horizon and the proportion of standard new-game starts. Evaluation games always begin from the standard new-game state.
_Avoid_: Synthetic board generation, evaluation shortcut

**Rollout Group**:
A set of candidate responses or trajectories compared by GRPO from the same start state and, for environment interaction, the same random-number seed. Different Rollout Groups use different seeds.
_Avoid_: Batch, evaluation seeds

**Proof of Learning**:
An exploratory Student Policy result with at least 10% 2048 Success Rate over the registered evaluation protocol.
_Avoid_: Project success

**Project Success**:
A Student Policy result with at least 50% 2048 Success Rate and no more than 1% Policy Failure Rate over the registered evaluation protocol.
_Avoid_: Proof of learning, teacher parity

**Strong Result**:
A Student Policy result with at least 90% 2048 Success Rate and no more than 1% Policy Failure Rate over the registered evaluation protocol.
_Avoid_: Project success, teacher parity

**2048 Success**:
A completed game in which the Student Policy produces a tile with value 2048 or greater.
_Avoid_: Win, solved game

**2048 Success Rate**:
The proportion of completed environment games that end in 2048 Success. It is the primary measure of Student Policy quality.
_Avoid_: Accuracy, teacher agreement

**Teacher Action Agreement**:
The proportion of held-out board states where the Student Policy selects the same action as the Teacher Policy. It is a diagnostic measure, not the final measure of game-playing quality.
_Avoid_: Win rate, policy quality
